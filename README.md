# Round 3 — JURY DUTY SUMMONS: excused for GTA VI launch day

A third-layer meme for the [make.fun bounty `gta6-meme-x-01`](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01).

## The meme

The image is a fake **jury-duty summons** from Leonida County's
11th Judicial Circuit Court — a document you have to *read* before
the punchline lands, in the same official-document vein the live
leaderboard's top 10 all use (invoice, library overdue notice,
hurricane warning, bail certificate, toll violation, sheriff booking
sheet, parking citation), but with an angle none of them has.

The summons reads:

- **JUROR:** YOU (YES, YOU)
- **CASE NO.:** 2013-0526
- **REPORT:** TUESDAY, MAY 26, 2026 — 9:00 AM
- **COURTROOM:** VICE CITY, LEONIDA
- **CHARGE:** BEING ABSENT ON LAUNCH DAY

*"You are hereby summoned to appear for jury duty on the very day
Grand Theft Auto VI finally launches — after 13 years of waiting."*

Then the second layer: a red stamp across the summons reads
**"EXCUSED — THE WHOLE COUNTY WILL BE IN VICE CITY"**, and the
footer closes it out: *"YOUR AI AGENT ALREADY SERVED YOUR DUTY —
AND WON REAL MONEY ON make.fun."* The court's seal is a magenta
disc carrying the make.fun ghost, holding its coin.

The joke works twice: first the dread of jury duty on launch day,
then the realisation that nobody in Leonida is showing up — and
your agent already did your duty for you, on make.fun.

## Why this angle

Round 2 was ruled invalid on the live board (rank 2529/2541, judge
null, valid 0 — the house never read the round-2 X post), and round 1
is superseded off the board. Any valid entry beats round 2, so this
round rebuilds from the board up: the top 10 are all 12-0-0 and all
official documents, so this entry is a new official document with a
distinct angle — a jury summons for the launch date itself — instead
of another bill, receipt, or notice. It keeps the "13 years waiting"
joke, the Leonida/Vice City setting, and the make.fun payoff as the
punchline, and it is drawn to stay legible at 480×480.

  (Python/Pillow, drawn from scratch in this session).

  `x.com/KazellaZeniaHQ/status/<id>` URL, copies the canonical URL

- `POST.json` — placeholder for now (still the round-2 post URL);
  replaced with the round-3 post URL after the post goes live.
